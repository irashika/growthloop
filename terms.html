Terms and Refund Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

The course

The AI Masterclass is a 30-day live online course taught in Tamil, with 90-minute classes. Dates, timings and class links are shared on WhatsApp.

Enrolment and payment

  • You pay by UPI QR code on the payment page and submit your UPI transaction ID.
  • Your seat is confirmed only after TheGrowthLoop checks the payment and replies to you on WhatsApp.
  • Seats are limited to 30 per cohort. Registration runs from 11 November to 30 November 2026. Prices can differ between cohorts.

Refund policy

We want you to join with confidence. Our refund rules are:

  • Before the first class: full refund (100%) of the fee you paid.
  • From the first class up to the end of Day 3: 50% refund of the fee you paid.
  • After Day 3: the fee is not refundable. You can still watch the recordings of classes you missed.
  • If TheGrowthLoop cancels or postpones the cohort: you can move to the next cohort or get a full refund.

To ask for a refund, message us on WhatsApp at +91 97919 96349 or email thegrowthloop.in@gmail.com with your name, WhatsApp number and UPI transaction ID. Approved refunds are sent to the UPI account you paid from within 7 working days. A promo code discount, such as NEWLEARNER, is not paid back as cash; refunds are calculated on the amount you actually paid.

Certificate

A TheGrowthLoop course completion certificate is given when you complete the daily tasks and attend at least 80% of the live classes. It is not a degree or a government qualification.

No guarantees

We teach skills and guide you in building a portfolio. We do not guarantee a job, a client, or any income.

Recordings and materials

Recordings and course materials are for your own learning. Please do not share or resell them.

Conduct

Please be respectful in classes and the community group. We may remove anyone who disrupts learning for others.

Changes

We may reschedule a class if needed and will inform you on WhatsApp. We may update these terms; the date above shows the latest version.
