What you'll learn

30 days, day by day.

Learn ChatGPT and other AI tools in Tamil. Tap a week to see every class.

Week 1 · Days 1–7: AI FoundationsUnderstand how AI works and write prompts that get professional-quality output.
  1. Day 1

    Welcome to the AI Era

    What AI is, AI vs ML vs Deep Learning vs Generative AI, and how AI is changing work.

    Hands-on: Set up ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini and Perplexity. Write a “My 30-day goal” post with AI.

  2. Day 2

    How AI Learns

    Data, training, models and neural networks, explained simply. What training and inference mean.

    Hands-on: Train a no-code image classifier with Teachable Machine and explain it in a 1-minute video.

  3. Day 3

    LLMs: Large Language Models

    Transformers, tokens, context window, why AI hallucinates, and GPT vs Claude vs Gemini vs Llama vs DeepSeek.

    Hands-on: Run one prompt on 4 LLMs and compare quality, speed and cost in a table.

  4. Day 4

    SLMs, Open-Source and Local AI

    Small vs large models, open vs closed models, and running AI on your own laptop.

    Hands-on: Run a small model locally with Ollama or LM Studio and test it offline.

  5. Day 5

    Prompt Engineering: Core

    Role, context, task, format and constraints. Zero-shot, one-shot and few-shot prompts.

    Hands-on: Build a personal library of 25 reusable prompts.

  6. Day 6

    Prompt Engineering: Advanced

    Chain-of-thought, reasoning modes, prompt chaining, system prompts, custom GPTs and Projects, prompt safety basics.

    Hands-on: Build a custom GPT or Project for a niche, such as a dental clinic content assistant.

  7. Day 7

    Multimodal AI + Week 1 Test

    Image understanding, image generation, voice AI and how diffusion models work.

    Hands-on: Quiz, then a mini-project: a brand kit (logo ideas, posts, voiceover) made only with AI.

Week 2 · Days 8–14: Advanced AI ConceptsBuild a RAG chatbot, an AI agent and a business automation.
  1. Day 8

    Embeddings and Vector Databases

    Semantic search vs keyword search, vector databases and chunking.

    Hands-on: Turn a set of FAQs into embeddings and search them by meaning.

  2. Day 9

    RAG: Retrieval-Augmented Generation

    How RAG reduces hallucinations, the RAG pipeline, and RAG vs fine-tuning vs long context.

    Hands-on: Build a “chat with your PDF” bot in NotebookLM, then a custom one with Flowise or n8n.

  3. Day 10

    Fine-Tuning, Customisation and AI APIs

    Fine-tuning basics, when to fine-tune vs prompt vs RAG, and using AI through an API.

    Hands-on: Make your first API call and build a small tool, such as a caption generator.

  4. Day 11

    AI Agents

    Agents vs chatbots vs workflows, tool use, memory, planning and multi-agent systems.

    Hands-on: Build a research agent that searches the web and writes a report.

  5. Day 12

    MCP and Connected AI

    What MCP is, connecting AI to Gmail, Drive, Notion and Slack, plus skills and plugins.

    Hands-on: Connect an AI assistant to Google Drive or Notion and automate a real task.

  6. Day 13

    AI Automation for Business

    n8n, Make and Zapier, triggers and webhooks, WhatsApp and Instagram bots, AI voice agents.

    Hands-on: Build a lead-capture, AI reply and Google Sheet automation for a local business.

  7. Day 14

    AI Ethics, Safety + Week 2 Test

    Fact-checking, bias, data privacy (India's DPDP Act basics), copyright, deepfakes and responsible use.

    Hands-on: Quiz, then a mini-project: a WhatsApp FAQ bot powered by RAG for a sample business.

Week 3 · Days 15–21: Create and Build with AIEdit video, design a brand kit and build a website with AI.
  1. Day 15

    AI Content Writing and Copywriting

    AIDA and PAS frameworks, blogs, SEO articles, ad copy, emails, brand voice and Tamil and Hindi content.

    Hands-on: Write a full content pack: 1 blog, 5 ad copies and 3 emails for a sample client.

  2. Day 16

    AI Video Editing: Short Form

    Reels and Shorts workflow, CapCut AI, Descript, auto-captions, hooks and AI avatars.

    Hands-on: Edit 3 Reels from raw footage using AI tools.

  3. Day 17

    AI Video Generation and Long Form

    Text-to-video, AI voiceover and dubbing, scripting and thumbnails with AI.

    Hands-on: Produce a 60-second AI-generated ad video with voiceover and music.

  4. Day 18

    AI for Design and Branding

    Canva AI, Adobe Firefly and Figma AI. Logos, posts, carousels, brand guidelines and UI/UX basics with AI.

    Hands-on: Design a social media kit: 10 posts and 3 carousels for a brand.

  5. Day 19

    Website Development with AI: No-Code

    Domains, hosting, SEO basics, AI website builders, WordPress with Elementor and landing pages.

    Hands-on: Build and publish a 5-page business website.

  6. Day 20

    Website Development with AI: Vibe Coding

    Lovable, Bolt, v0, Cursor and Claude Code, HTML, CSS and JS basics, and deploying live.

    Hands-on: Build a custom web app, such as an appointment booking page, and deploy it.

  7. Day 21

    AI Marketing, SEO + Week 3 Test

    AI for SEO and AI search, Meta and Google Ads with AI, social scheduling, analytics and market research.

    Hands-on: Quiz, then a mini-project: a full digital presence (site, social kit and 1 ad) for a mock client.

Week 4 · Days 22–28: Earn with AISet up your freelance profile, price your services and prepare your client pitches.
  1. Day 22

    AI Income Map

    Ways to use AI skills: freelancing, an AI agency, content, digital products, consulting and automation services. Picking a niche.

    Hands-on: Choose a niche and 3 core services. Write a one-page business plan.

  2. Day 23

    Building a Portfolio That Sells

    Case-study format, before and after showcases, a portfolio site with AI, and personal branding on LinkedIn and Instagram.

    Hands-on: Publish a portfolio site with your Week 2 and Week 3 projects as case studies.

  3. Day 24

    Freelance Platforms

    Fiverr gigs, Upwork profiles and proposals, LinkedIn, Contra, Freelancer and Instagram as a client channel.

    Hands-on: Create a Fiverr gig and an Upwork profile. Send 5 proposals.

  4. Day 25

    Getting Clients Directly

    Local business outreach, cold email and WhatsApp scripts, LinkedIn outreach, free audit offers and referrals.

    Hands-on: Build a 50-lead list and send 15 personalised pitches.

  5. Day 26

    Pricing, Proposals and Closing

    Pricing models, packages, proposals and quotes with AI, discovery calls and handling objections.

    Hands-on: Create a pricing sheet and a proposal template. Practise a mock sales call.

  6. Day 27

    Delivering Like a Pro

    Client onboarding, briefs and contracts, scope creep, revisions, project management, invoicing, and GST and freelancer tax basics.

    Hands-on: Build a client onboarding kit: brief form, contract and invoice template.

  7. Day 28

    Scaling: Freelancer to AI Agency

    Productising services, retainers, hiring and white-labelling, SOPs with AI and building an audience.

    Hands-on: Write a 90-day growth plan. Review each other's offers.

Final · Days 29–30: Capstone and Demo DayDeliver a complete client-style project and present it live.
  1. Day 29

    Capstone Build Day

    Pick a real or mock local business and build a full AI package: website, social media kit, short video ad and one automation or chatbot. Mentors review live.

    Output: a finished client package and a case study.

  2. Day 30

    Demo Day and Graduation

    A 5-minute pitch to a panel, feedback, a career roadmap session (jobs vs freelancing vs agency) and the certificate ceremony.

    Output: a recorded pitch for your portfolio, and your certificate.
